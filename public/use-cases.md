# KRW.O Use Cases

KRW.O is built for filing-grounded U.S. public company research. The product is Korean-first, but the research workflow is useful to any investor or AI agent that needs company answers backed by original disclosures.

## 1. 10-K and 10-Q Revenue Analysis

Use KRW.O when a user wants to understand why a company's revenue changed. The system is designed to connect the question to company context, business lines, segments, management discussion, and risk language instead of only summarizing the filing.

Example questions:

- Why did NVIDIA data center revenue grow, and what company comments support it?
- How are Apple's iPhone, Services, Mac, iPad, and Wearables revenue streams changing?
- Which business lines explain Amazon's margin improvement?

Best fit:
Questions about revenue drivers, segment mix, recurring revenue, pricing, volume, backlog, customer concentration, and management commentary.

## 2. Margin, Cost Structure, and Cash-Flow Research

KRW.O is useful when the user wants to know whether a business trend is actually showing up in margins or cash flow.

Example questions:

- Is AI infrastructure spending pressuring free cash flow?
- Are gross margin gains coming from mix, pricing, or cost discipline?
- Does capex growth look like a temporary investment phase or a structural cash-flow drag?

Best fit:
Questions about gross margin, operating margin, R&D, SBC, capex, operating cash flow, free cash flow, debt, and capital allocation.

## 3. Risk Factors and Thesis Check

KRW.O can help turn a thesis into a filing-grounded checklist. The goal is not to produce a guaranteed investment decision, but to identify what would support, weaken, or break the thesis.

Example questions:

- What risks would break the bull case for this company?
- Does the latest 10-Q support the AI growth thesis?
- What company comments should I monitor before buying or holding?

Best fit:
Investment-decision questions, thesis validation, risk factor comparison, scenario questions, and follow-up research prompts.

## 4. 13F Holdings to Company Research

KRW.O includes public 13F manager pages for selected institutional filers. A 13F page should be used as a starting point, not as a buy signal.

Example workflow:

1. Review a manager page such as Berkshire Hathaway, BlackRock, ARK Invest, or National Pension Service.
2. Identify top holdings, new positions, increased weights, or reduced weights.
3. Open the related company page.
4. Ask whether the company's filings support the implied business thesis.

Example questions:

- Which Berkshire holdings have the strongest filing-backed cash-flow evidence?
- Which ARK holdings show real revenue conversion from AI or software demand?
- Which BlackRock top holdings are most exposed to company-specific risk?

## 5. AI-Agent-Ready Filing Database

KRW.O is designed so AI agents can work from structured company context rather than raw PDFs. The internal workflow starts from company, filing period, business context, risk, and evidence path.

Useful agent tasks:

- Search supported company context before writing an answer
- Retrieve filing-grounded evidence for a specific company question
- Compare company drivers across supported tickers
- Preserve a research trail for follow-up questions

## Limits

- KRW.O only covers supported companies and indexed filings.
- 13F data is delayed and does not show short positions, private holdings, or all derivatives.
- The product is intended for research and verification, not personalized investment advice.
- Users should treat outputs as a starting point for judgment, not as a final trading instruction.
