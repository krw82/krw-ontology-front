# KRW.O Pricing

KRW.O uses simple monthly subscription plans with weekly research capacity. The product is designed for filing-grounded U.S. public company research, source-checkable answers, and repeated follow-up questions.

Public pricing page:
https://krwontology.com/ko/pricing

## Free

- Price: KRW 0/month
- Best for: first analysis and lightweight product evaluation
- Typical weekly capacity: about 5 standard questions
- Includes: all research modes, including wide research; company search; filing-grounded answers; source context; follow-up questions; and up to three saved companies

## Lite

- Price: KRW 2,900/month
- Best for: users who want to keep the core research flow active more often
- Typical weekly capacity: about 15 standard questions
- Includes: everything in Free with expanded analysis capacity

## Standard

- Price: KRW 7,900/month
- Best for: individual investors who regularly research U.S. companies
- Typical weekly capacity: about 41 standard questions
- Includes: every research mode and more analysis capacity than Lite

## Pro

- Price: KRW 29,000/month
- Best for: users who compare multiple companies or run frequent research workflows
- Typical weekly capacity: about 207 standard questions
- Includes: the highest analysis capacity plus the research notebook for maintaining theses, questions, notes, and review conditions

## How Usage Works

- Billing is monthly.
- Research capacity refills weekly from the subscription anchor.
- Usage depends on research scope and answer generation, not only the number of questions.
- If a weekly limit is reached, users can wait for refill or upgrade.

## Product Scope

KRW.O is strongest when the user asks company questions that can be checked against 10-K, 10-Q, company context, risk factors, business drivers, cash flow, and 13F holdings context.

Examples:

- "Does the latest 10-Q support the AI growth thesis?"
- "Why did margin improve, and what company comments explain it?"
- "Which 13F holding should I research first?"
- "What would make this investment thesis weaker?"

## Important Limits

- KRW.O is not an automated investment advisor.
- It does not guarantee investment outcomes.
- It does not replace user judgment.
- Coverage depends on supported companies and indexed filing data.
- 13F holdings are delayed public disclosures and should not be treated as current buy or sell instructions.
