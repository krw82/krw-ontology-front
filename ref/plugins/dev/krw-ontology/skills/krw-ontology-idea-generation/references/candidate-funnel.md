# Candidate Funnel

Use this funnel to turn broad candidate relevance into a bounded deep-research queue.

## Contents

```text
Funnel
Candidate Route
Exposure
Recent Evidence
Financial Pathway
Offset And First Rejection
Classification
```

## Funnel

```text
1. Candidate route
2. Direct or operational exposure
3. Recent filing evidence
4. Financial pathway
5. Offset and first rejection
6. A / B / C / Reject
```

## 1. Candidate Route

The v3 global spine may surface companies related to the internal idea-screen brief.

This is discovery only.

```text
candidate ranking != evidence strength
keyword relevance != economic exposure
multiple search hits != investable idea
```

## 2. Exposure

Prefer evidence that the company:

```text
sells the relevant product or service
serves the relevant customer or end market
owns the relevant asset or capacity
has orders, backlog, contracts, pricing, or volume tied to the driver
faces the requested cost, demand, regulatory, or capital-allocation channel
```

Separate:

```text
direct exposure
operationally related exposure
thematic association only
```

## 3. Recent Evidence

Start with the latest available filing.

Use:

```text
latest 10-Q for current drivers and changes
latest 10-K for annual business mix and structural baseline
older filings for persistence or cycle comparison
```

Do not advance a candidate primarily from stale or generic language.

## 4. Financial Pathway

State the pathway explicitly:

```text
driver
-> orders / demand / pricing / volume / capacity
-> revenue / margin / cash flow / balance sheet
-> investor meaning
```

If the middle step is missing, the pathway is not proven.

## 5. Offset And First Rejection

Every serious candidate needs:

```text
the strongest offsetting burden
the earliest evidence that could break the idea
```

Examples:

```text
demand benefit offset by capex and financing burden
backlog growth offset by weak conversion or margin
revenue exposure offset by customer concentration
volume growth offset by pricing pressure
theme exposure offset by a small or unquantified business contribution
```

## 6. Classification

```text
A:
direct exposure + recent evidence + financial pathway + explicit rejection risk

B:
credible exposure and pathway, but one material condition or evidence gap remains

C:
theme relevance exists, but directness, recency, or financial linkage is weak

Reject:
the candidate fails exposure proof or is a clear false positive for this screen
```
