## Typography

- Body: `"Pretendard", "Apple SD Gothic Neo", "Malgun Gothic", -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, sans-serif`
- Metadata: `"SFMono-Regular", Menlo, Consolas, ui-monospace, monospace`
- The existing body family is retained for Korean legibility. No unverified external font is added to a local file prototype.

## Signature detail

- A thin blue editorial rule and compact mono timestamp provide the 120% detail: evidence reads like a legible record, not an AI-generated card.

## Guardrails

- No gradients, dark “AI” surfaces, rounded white cards with coloured left rails, decorative icons, or black CTA blocks.
- One accent colour only: the existing KRW.O blue.
- Company/event content must stay text-first; company logos are intentionally not invented in a direction prototype.

## Product character

Quiet, evidence-led, compact, editorial, decisive.
